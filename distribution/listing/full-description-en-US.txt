Find your flow with Tempo — the calm habit tracker and daily planner that puts today on one screen.

Tempo isn't another endless checklist. Focus shows only what's due today, with anything overdue and the next task worth starting. Routines keep your habits in rhythm with <b>Habit Chains</b>, and Tasks hold everything else, sorted the way you think. Whether you're building a morning routine, quitting a habit or planning your day, Tempo stays out of your way so you can get things done.

<b>🔗 CORE FEATURES</b>

🎯 <b>Focus — today, not everything</b>
Tasks due today and anything overdue, together in one place, with the work worth starting next up front. Loose ends with no date? Give them one from Focus without losing your place.

⏱️ <b>Focus sessions — work in sessions</b>
Start a timer on any task with a single tap. Tempo counts it down, tells you when it's time for a break, and keeps the countdown with you as you move around the app. Choose how long each session lasts, and mark the task done, pause or stop whenever you like.

🔄 <b>Habit Chains — keep the rhythm</b>
Link habits that flow one into the next, so your "Morning Routine" is one chain instead of scattered reminders. Track habits to build and habits to quit, and watch your streaks grow — the habit tracker for people who think in routines, not isolated checkboxes.

✅ <b>Tasks — clear your head, plan your day</b>
Capture to-dos in seconds, then organize them with categories, sections, priorities and due dates. A lightweight task manager and daily planner for a busy life.

🎨 <b>Make Tempo feel like yours</b>
Built on Material You, with expressive motion. Start with Tempo's signature palette or match your wallpaper on supported devices, in light or dark.

🛡️ <b>Private and offline</b>
Tempo works completely offline and encrypts your data on your device — including anything you export as a backup. Your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no ads, no tracking.
